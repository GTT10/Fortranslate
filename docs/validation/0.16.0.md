# PeleF 0.16.0 validation record

Base: PeleF 0.15.0 reactive two-dimensional characteristic-PPM branch.

## New subsystem

- seven-species Lennard--Jones transport database;
- Chapman--Enskog viscosity and binary diffusion;
- Wilke mixture viscosity;
- modified-Eucken/Mathur conductivity;
- mixture-averaged species diffusion;
- 1D viscous stress, Fourier flux, barodiffusion, correction velocity, and
  species-enthalpy energy flux;
- explicit SSPRK2 transport and parabolic timestep control;
- symmetric reaction/transport/hydro operator composition;
- transport coefficient probe and live Cantera qualification;
- analytical shear-wave, species-smoothing, thermal-smoothing, and coupled
  transport-pulse regressions.

## Cantera 3.2 coefficient qualification

The four-state probe compares this deliberately simplified dilute-gas subset
with Cantera's mixture-averaged transport implementation. The measured maximum
relative errors are:

| Coefficient | Maximum error | Worst state | Qualification bound |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Mixture viscosity | `2.94866223e-2` | `product_1500K` | `5%` |
| Thermal conductivity | `8.88043371e-2` | `lean_1800K_5atm` | `10%` |
| Mixture-averaged diffusion | `3.79177529e-1` | `stoich_600K:H2` | `40%` |

The bounds are intentionally evidence-based and asymmetric. Viscosity and
conductivity agree much more closely than species diffusion. The larger H2
diffusion discrepancy is retained and documented rather than hidden; the
current model omits the full fitted/polar collision treatment used by Cantera
and PelePhysics.

## Scope qualification

The implementation is a dilute ideal-gas mixture-transport subset. Cantera is
used to bound coefficient error, not to claim exact transport parity. Soret,
Dufour, bulk viscosity, polar corrections, multicomponent diffusion, and all
2D molecular-transport coupling remain outside this record.
