# PeleF 0.221.0 validation record

## Scope

This milestone adds an optional production-stiff integration boundary to the
configure-time selected serial 0D application. `integrator = "implicit"`
remains the default. `integrator = "cvode"` uses SUNDIALS 7.2.0 CVODE BDF
through the official Fortran interface, a serial N_Vector, dense SUNMatrix,
and dense linear solver. The adapter is a separate static runtime and does not
enter fixed applications, MPI targets, or the selected mechanism probe.

The qualification source is the recursive PeleC baseline: SUNDIALS commit
`0eff39663606f2ff280c4059a947ed62ae38180a`, identified by its source
`CMakeLists.txt` as version 7.2.0. It was built locally with the official
Fortran module interface enabled, double precision, 32-bit SUNDIALS indices,
CVODE enabled, and other solver families disabled. CMake requires exact
version 7.2.0 plus the `fcvode_mod`, `fnvecserial_mod`,
`fsunmatrixdense_mod`, and `fsunlinsoldense_mod` components. The option is
`OFF` by default, and a C-only, shared-library, or different-version prefix is
not accepted. Requiring static imported targets keeps the selected installed
executable independent of a non-installed SUNDIALS runtime library.

## State, Jacobian, and failure contract

- CVODE owns a private `N-1` state. The largest initial mass-fraction species
  is fixed as dependent and reconstructed from exact closure in every
  callback.
- The RHS is the existing fixed-density, fixed-specific-internal-energy
  generic reactor RHS. Temperature is recovered algebraically and is not a
  CVODE component.
- The dense Jacobian is the generalized energy-constrained reduced Jacobian.
  Its kinetics composition derivative is analytic; `d(rhs)/dT` is a bounded
  centered finite difference, so the complete callback is semi-analytic.
- Independent species carry nonnegative CVODE constraints. Nonfinite,
  negative, over-unity, or closure-invalid trials return a recoverable
  callback error.
- Caller `Y` and `T` are updated only after the requested time, composition,
  energy recovery, and cumulative internal-step gates pass. A failed internal
  advance leaves them byte-unchanged and quarantines the handle until cleanup.
- The current module-private mechanism context permits one active reactor per
  process. Nested initialization is rejected; finalization is idempotent. The
  boundary is serial, non-reentrant, and not thread safe.
- `maximum_steps` remains the native implicit limit.
  `cvode_max_internal_steps` is the separate cumulative CVODE limit. A final
  scheduler fragment shorter than `minimum_time_step` temporarily lowers the
  CVODE minimum only to that requested fragment.

## Direct numerical evidence

The generalized reduced Jacobian passed its centered directional gate both
with the final species dependent and with species 1 dependent. The dedicated
CVODE API unit test forced a one-internal-step failure, verified exact caller
state rollback, finalized the failed handle, rejected nested initialization,
calibrated and exhausted a cumulative step budget across output calls without
performing an extra internal step, rejected a nonmonotonic target without state
mutation, observed positive internal/RHS/Jacobian/nonlinear counters, and
finalized twice successfully.

The two-species fixture deliberately makes bundle species 1 (`H2`) the
largest mass fraction, so it exercises the nonfinal dependent-species map.
Two runs each used 6 CVODE internal steps, 9 RHS evaluations, 1 Jacobian
evaluation, and 8 nonlinear iterations, with no nonlinear convergence or
error-test failure. Across five rows to `1.0e-7 s`:

- density drift and closure error were exactly zero;
- maximum relative internal-energy error was
  `2.4690138609422258e-16`;
- maximum hydrogen-inventory error was
  `1.1102230246251565e-16`;
- temperature changed by `2.5858208655446333 K` and the largest mass-fraction
  change was `1.3159284177721720e-4`;
- both CSV files had SHA-256
  `9dd0b0a2b31932ebc4926874178fc0b33511f454a101b5c293a22848b3ae544c`.

The CVODE scheduler also reached exactly `1.000005e-7 s` and emitted all four
rows when the last `5.0e-13 s` output fragment was below the configured
`1.0e-12 s` minimum.

The pinned 10-species/29-reaction H2/O2 CVODE case used `rtol=1.0e-8` and
`atol=1.0e-14`, emitted 101 rows to `2.0e-3 s`, and used 1,233 internal steps,
1,738 RHS calls, 24 Jacobian calls, and 1,737 nonlinear iterations. Structural
checks observed:

- zero density drift and maximum closure `2.2204460492503131e-16`;
- maximum relative internal-energy error `9.3780706296730739e-12`;
- H/O inventory errors at most `6.9388939039072284e-17`;
- no negative mass fraction and no AR drift;
- final `T=2906.5221082957737 K` and `p=262444.24828748847 Pa`;
- CSV SHA-256
  `ef9843aa87edfd9bdbb6cdba1e0afa1e9fd394880254fcfc3f502ae7a022f5ef`.

Against Cantera 3.2.0 `IdealGasReactor` at the same initial composition,
constant volume, output times, and pinned `ohmech` YAML, the worst temperature
error was `0.0388316948492502 K` at `0.22 ms`, the worst pressure error was
`3.1558133272046689 Pa` at `0.22 ms`, and the worst species absolute error was
`1.2730400806513489e-8` for O at `0.20 ms`. Their configured scaled errors
were `0.359`, `0.307`, and `0.375`. Instantaneous production rates were
evaluated by Cantera at each exact PeleF state; the worst scaled rate error was
`0.549`, with maximum absolute error `5.4919348582704863e-11 kmol/(m^3 s)`.

## Qualification boundary

This is numerical integration, conservation, deterministic-repeat, failure,
and pinned Cantera evidence for one configure-time selected serial 0D
mechanism. It is not runtime YAML/JSON parsing, selected-mechanism CFD,
transport, AMR, EB, MPI, or spray coupling. It does not qualify mechanisms
above 32 species, rate families outside the current schema, concurrent or
thread-safe contexts, sparse linear algebra, sensitivity analysis, detailed
fuels, experiment parity, or production performance. The repository license
remains an explicit owner decision and release blocker.
