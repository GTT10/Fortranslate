## Implemented changes and failures resolved

The 53-species/268-reaction Lu mechanism could not previously be imported:
its C2H3 dissociation uses Troe A=1.9816 and T2=-0.0795 K, and several other
reactions use A=1. The importer/generator and runtime now preserve finite A
and signed T2, consistent with the expression in
[Cantera's Troe implementation](https://github.com/Cantera/cantera/blob/v3.2.0/src/kinetics/Falloff.cpp).
T1/T3 remain positive; an evaluated nonpositive center or overflowing T2
exponential fails explicitly. Existing zero/omitted T2 semantics remain.

The first stoichiometric run at 1100 K, 4 MPa failed with CVODE status -4 at
0.233892 ms. Temporary diagnostics identified negative Newton trial species
(e.g. -6.85e-10 mass fraction) rejected by the physical-state guard. The RHS
and Jacobian callbacks now use a nonnegative extension of independent trial
coordinates, reconstructing the dependent species to close the sum. Jacobian
columns for clipped coordinates are zero. Initialization and returned states
still use the original constraints and conservation checks; no acceptance
tolerance was relaxed. Diagnostic code was removed.

After that fix, the stoichiometric trajectory reached the common NASA7 upper
bound of 3000 K at 0.235357 ms and correctly failed with status -10. The final
fixture therefore uses phi=0.5 and runs through ignition and post-ignition
within the source's thermodynamic interval. This does not authorize NASA7
extrapolation above 3000 K or demonstrate a broad ignition envelope.
