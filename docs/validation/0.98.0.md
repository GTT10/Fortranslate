## Scope

This milestone adds static three-level EB composite integration and
deepest-to-root average-down for generic and reactive conserved fields over one
strictly nested pair of aligned rectangles.

## Automated evidence

The generic gate builds an 8 by 8 plane-EB root, a ratio-two 12 by 12 middle
level, and a nested ratio-two 16 by 16 finest level. Distinct constant fields
on each level exercise exact composite ownership across regular, cut, and
covered cells. The synchronized root integral must match the pre-sync
three-level composite integral; uncovered root and middle cells remain
unchanged, and the finest constant restricts exactly into the middle patch.

The reactive gate applies distinct conservative scalings at all three levels.
It requires componentwise composite conservation, finite positive
EOS-recovered temperatures on both parents, and unchanged unrefined ownership.
A nonfinite finest state and a nonpositive finest temperature independently
require full root-and-middle rollback.

## Qualification boundary

milestone does not allocate or prolong a lifecycle hierarchy and does not add
