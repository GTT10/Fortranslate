# PeleF 0.244.0 validation record

## Scope

This milestone adds mixture molecular transport to the existing fixed and
configure-time selected, static, strictly interior, periodic, two-level 3D AMR
hierarchy in serial and rank-local sparse MPI execution. It retains the
established hydro, chemistry, selected-context, and transport-disabled restart
paths.

## Numerical contract

The hierarchy transport step selects `min(dt_coarse, r^2 dt_fine)`. Each
transport Euler stage advances the coarse level once and the fine level through
`r^2` subcycles with time-interpolated, limited-PLM coarse ghosts. The six fine
boundary fluxes are averaged over child faces and time, limited against the
uncovered coarse species budgets, applied with an equal-and-opposite fine-side
correction, refluxed, and averaged down before temperature recovery. Two Euler
stages form SSPRK2. Active chemistry and transport compose transactionally as
`R-T-H-T-R`.

Sparse MPI keeps parent-aligned coarse and fine x slabs rank-local. Ranks with
no fine planes participate in transport provenance, policy, limiter, reflux,
and final-acceptance collectives. State, temperature, theta, and reflux
diagnostics publish only after the complete candidate succeeds.

## Focused qualification

The GNU Debug/Release serial and GNU/OpenMPI Debug/Release MPI focused gates
cover:

- individual viscosity, thermal-conduction, and species-diffusion modes;
- refinement ratios two and three and the `r^2` timestep/subcycle contract;
- active x/y/z lower/upper coarse/fine species limiting, nonnegative species,
  EOS recovery, and composite conservation;
- external theta values zero and one plus transactional rejection of negative,
  greater-than-one, and NaN values;
- barodiffusion-without-species-diffusion configuration and API rejection;
- active serial `R-T-H-T-R` and rollback of state, temperature, theta, and
  reflux diagnostics after a deliberately rejected middle hydro stage;
- bit-exact serial/sparse transport timestep, diffusivity, state, temperature,
  limiter, and reflux at one, two, four, and eight ranks;
- all six active limiter orientations and active `R-T-H-T-R` at one, two,
  four, and eight ranks, including zero-fine-plane ranks;
- rank-local gas-transport metadata mismatch rejection without mutation.

## Clean installed Release

An isolated tests-disabled GNU/OpenMPI Release build installs 42 executables.
All 42 installed ELF files are byte-identical to their build-tree sources, use
non-executable stacks, have no RPATH/RUNPATH or unresolved dependency, and link
only the expected OpenMPI ABI 40 libraries.

Installed fixed and selected transport applications produce byte-identical
coarse/fine results in serial and at one, two, four, and eight ranks; the
eight-rank runs include ranks with zero fine planes. Installed
transport-disabled selected schema-3 restart remains exact from one serial
checkpoint to serial and one-/two-/four-/eight-rank MPI continuations. Fixed
schema-2 serial restart and four-/eight-rank continuation also retain their
frozen hashes. The complete audit is frozen in
[`evidence/0.244.0-clean-release-install-audit.txt`](evidence/0.244.0-clean-release-install-audit.txt).

## Qualification boundary

This is short-time structural, conservation, transactional, and deterministic
rank-parity evidence for one static two-level hierarchy. It does not qualify
transport persistence, dynamic 3D topology, boundary-touching refinement,
physical coarse boundaries, 3D EB AMR, scalable I/O, performance/scaling,
detailed-fuel physical validation, ignition, or external PeleC field parity.
Repository licensing remains a separate project-owner release decision.
