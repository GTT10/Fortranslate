# PeleF 0.212.0 validation record

## Scope

This milestone adds conservative first-order FluxRedist to the qualified
single-level, static, axis-plane 3D EB kernel. It supplies a standalone
right-hand-side redistribution, a transactional general-EOS update from that
residual, and an end-to-end PCM EB Euler path that redistributes the
aperture/wall-flux divergence before temperature recovery.

## Automated evidence

- fully regular geometry leaves an arbitrary residual unchanged, while fully
  covered geometry returns exactly zero;
- x-, y-, and z-normal `kappa=0.05` cells independently check the analytical
  cut and receiving-cell residuals, compact support, stiffness reduction, and
  every component's fluid-volume-weighted conservation;
- a `8 x 3 x 3` x-normal cut sheet verifies both y neighbors, both z
  neighbors, and the positive-x regular neighbor, including overlapping
  neighborhood conservation;
- a uniform active residual is preserved exactly and invalid geometry or a
  nonfinite residual is rejected without publishing a partial result;
- the low-level reactive update converts a raw negative-density step into a
  positive cut/neighbor pair, recovers temperature, conserves every NASA7
  Euler/species component, and rolls back a still-nonphysical request;
- the end-to-end regression repeats exact uniform tangent-flow invariance for
  all three plane orientations and Riemann solvers through the redistributed
  path;
- a `kappa=0.05` density jump is advanced at a three-dimensional CFL-0.5
  full-grid timestep: the independently formed raw residual gives negative
  cut density and the raw transactional update rejects it, while FluxRedist
  completes with positive state and roundoff conservation;
- the preceding nonuniform small-step conservation, species closure, covered
  identity, and invalid solver/timestep/state rollback gates run through both
  raw and redistributed Euler paths.

## Numerical signatures

The one-neighbor analytical case reduces the cut-cell residual magnitude to
`0.0952380952380952` of the raw value at `kappa=0.05`. In the Release
end-to-end gate, the CFL-0.5 full-grid timestep is
`2.7268814571340861e-05`; the redistributed cut density is
`8.7257250000000008e-01`, whereas the raw candidate density is negative.

Across the raw and redistributed nonuniform regressions, maximum relative
fluid-volume conservation error is `2.7755575615628914e-17`, maximum species
closure error is `5.5511151231257827e-17`, and all x/y/z uniform state and
temperature errors are exactly zero in the GNU Fortran 13.3.0 Release build.

## Qualification boundary

This record qualifies six-face first-order FluxRedist for the exact axis-plane
geometry and the current serial, single-level, PCM, inviscid general-EOS Euler
operator. The outer Cartesian boundary remains zero-gradient. The raw update
is retained only as a diagnostic/reference route.

It does not qualify weighted or higher-order StateRedist, arbitrary geometry,
an EB-aware public timestep selector, characteristic EB reconstruction,
chemistry, molecular transport, AMR/reflux/regridding, checkpoint/restart,
MPI ownership, scalable I/O, a public 3D EB application, or whole-application
PeleC parity.
