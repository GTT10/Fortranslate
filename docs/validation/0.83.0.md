# PeleF 0.83.0 validation record

## Scope

This milestone adds normalized Cartesian open-face centroids, a selectable
active-stencil frozen-composition characteristic-PLM predictor, and
PeleC-ordered face-center Godunov then tangential face-centroid flux staging to
the runnable serial reactive EB path.

## Automated evidence

The geometry unit gate checks zero offsets on regular and covered faces, the
analytical `+0.35` normalized centroid of a vertical-plane partial y-face, the
sign and magnitude relation `abs(c)=0.5*(1-area_fraction)` on diagonal-plane
partial faces, and rejection of an invalid offset. A synthetic tangentially
linear face flux is interpolated to both x- and y-face centroids.

The hydro unit gate constructs an affine moving contact at constant pressure
and velocity. Characteristic PLM must reproduce its analytical time-centered
x- and y-mass fluxes while differing from PCM. Uniform pressure then exercises
PCM and PLM through regular, vertical-plane, diagonal-plane, and circular-wall
flux, divergence, StateRedist, and EOS recovery. Unknown limiters and solvers
retain zero-output or unchanged-state transaction contracts. The committed
circular-obstacle executable case now selects `characteristic_plm`.

Implementation commits `230d24d`, `07426d3`, and `5448ae1` are qualified by
branch CI in GNU Fortran Debug and Release configurations. The full suite
contains 150 tests.

## Qualification boundary

This covers serial single-level normal characteristic PLM, linear
face-centroid flux interpolation, zero-gradient outer boundaries, stationary
adiabatic slip walls, active-cell chemistry, and zeroth-order weighted
StateRedist. It does not include an unsplit EB transverse predictor, PPM,
higher-order StateRedist, EB molecular transport, periodic EB neighborhoods,
thermal/viscous/catalytic or moving walls, AMR coupling, or MPI distribution.
