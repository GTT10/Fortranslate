## Contract

The caller supplies old/new nonoverlapping coarse-index patch boxes, their
refinement ratios, old fine states, new fine geometries and a fixed coarse
geometry/state. Empty old/new patch arrays allow refinement from a coarse-only
state or complete coarsening. Old and destination patch arrays must be distinct.

The remapper validates both layouts and matches coarsened patch metrics to
the parent section. Old fine states are fluid-volume restricted into a private
coarse candidate, including EOS validation. Exposed coarse cells are also
validated and their temperature recovered. Piecewise-constant prolongation
initializes new fine regions. At unchanged ratio, overlapping old/new fine
regions require matching metrics and reuse state and temperature exactly.
At changed ratio, the conservative coarse donor is used; old subcell variation
is deliberately not reconstructed across resolutions.

All states publish together only after every layout, geometry and state check
passes. A failed operation preserves coarse state/temperature and destination
patch fields. Coarse cells under fine patches retain their volume averages.
No boundary or reaction exchange arises from regridding. This is fixed-wall
regridding, not moving-wall geometry or a cut-cell intersection remapper.
