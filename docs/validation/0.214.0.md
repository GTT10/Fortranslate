# PeleF 0.214.0 validation record

## Scope

This milestone adds a full-grid reactive EB CFL selector and the installed
`pelef_reactive_eb_3d` multi-step application. The public input requires an
exact x/y/z plane with a regular receiver and selects either zeroth-order
StateRedist or first-order FluxRedist; raw unstabilized advancement is not a
public option.

## Automated evidence

- the CFL unit gate reproduces the analytical three-direction spectral rate,
  ignores invalid covered storage, and rejects an invalid active state, bad
  CFL number, or all-covered domain transactionally;
- the configuration unit gate accepts x/y/z planes and a real namelist read,
  while rejecting raw advancement, invalid target, nonfinite data,
  face-aligned planes, cut cells without a regular receiver, and a
  StateRedist target that would not activate the merge;
- both public cases build `10 x 8 x 6` geometry with 144 covered, 48 cut, and
  288 regular cells, then take two full-grid-CFL steps to `5.0e-5`;
- application diagnostics volume-weight the state and gate mass, energy, all
  species, and tangential momenta while reporting wall-normal momentum as an
  external wall exchange;
- the output writer records 480 x-fastest rows with indices, Cartesian
  centers, cell type, `kappa`, physical fluid centroids, primitives, and all
  `Y_k`/`rhoY_k` fields;
- the independent checker reconstructs the planar geometry, composition and
  ideal-mixture relation, analytical mass/species integrals, tangential
  momentum, resolved density/pressure response, frozen method signatures, and
  cross-method energy/species agreement.

## Numerical signatures

Both public runs use first step `2.7268814571340861e-05` and clipped second
step `2.2731185428659142e-05`.

StateRedist reports maximum invariant conservation error
`2.1256253963650108e-15`, maximum density
`3.5148453758607451e-01`, and wall-normal momentum change
`-6.6722839159430808e-02`. FluxRedist reports
`1.4879377774555075e-15`, `5.7127335250979783e-01`, and
`6.5149901427028103e-02`, respectively. Their differing wall response is
expected because the algorithms redistribute different quantities.

The independent CSV checker reports exact cross-method energy-invariant
agreement at its printed precision, correct 480-row geometry, and both frozen
maximum-density signatures.

## Qualification boundary

This record qualifies a serial, single-level, inviscid, PCM, forward-Euler
public hydro workflow for one exact non-face-aligned axis plane, the frozen
elementary H2/O2/N2 mixture, zero-gradient Cartesian boundaries, and either
order-zero StateRedist or first-order FluxRedist. The output is deterministic
CSV, not scalable production I/O.

It does not qualify general geometry, higher-order temporal or EB spatial
reconstruction, chemistry, molecular transport, AMR/reflux/regridding,
checkpoint/restart, MPI ownership, external field parity, or physical
validation against PeleC or experiment.
