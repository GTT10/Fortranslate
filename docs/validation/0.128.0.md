# PeleF 0.128.0 validation record

## Scope

This milestone adds root-owner temperature tagging and accepted-step scheduled
topology rebuilding to the public sparse MPI reactive EB AMR time loop.

## Automated evidence

The gate starts from two separated ratio-two children and a
thermodynamically consistent root temperature hotspot. An independent serial
loop performs two dynamically selected full-physics steps and evaluates the
shared temperature-gradient planner at step two. The sparse public loop must
match its exact time, step, regrid-evaluation and topology-change counts,
minimum timestep, limiter, ordered topology, root fields, and every resulting
child field.

Root-gather sends for both stability selection and the scheduled plan are
checked against an owner-map formula at one, two, four, and eight ranks. A
second run rejects the geometry callback after a valid physics candidate; the
clock, step, distribution, sparse fields, geometry template, counters, and
published traffic must remain at their pre-step values.

CI qualification details are recorded after the implementation head passes
both workflows.

## Qualification boundary

Tag planning is root-owner-only and broadcasts compact topology metadata, but
all ranks reconstruct geometry and the topology transfer retains the
replicated compatibility window. Sparse checkpoint/restart, parallel output,
replica-free overlap migration, arbitrary-depth dynamic EB topology, and
decomposed root physics remain outside this milestone.
