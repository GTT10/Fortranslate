# Stationary adiabatic no-slip walls in regular 3D

Parent: `dfc8ea9`. `no_slip_wall` adds viscous wall shear through odd reflection
of all velocity components for transport. Euler ghosts retain normal-only
reflection, preventing artificial tangential drag from the Rusanov flux.
The existing split-step surface ledger includes viscous wall momentum transfer.
The stationary adiabatic wall transfers neither mass nor energy.

GNU Fortran 13.3.0 Debug, `build/port-spray-debug`:

```sh
cmake --build build/port-spray-debug --parallel 8 --target \
  test_reactive_boundaries_3d test_reactive_boundary_transport_3d pelef_reactive_3d
ctest --test-dir build/port-spray-debug --output-on-failure \
  -R '^(unit_reactive_(boundaries_3d|boundary_transport_3d)|reactive_boundary_3d_no_slip)$'
```

Three distinct tests passed. The hydro test covers both wall types with
PCM/PLM and three Riemann solvers. The transport test verifies exact initial
wall shear `-4 mu U A dt/dy` between two stationary walls, absence of artificial
Euler wall friction, kinetic-to-internal energy conversion, closure, positivity,
surface budgets and rollback. The application example's normalized Euler
residual was `1.665e-16`; gas x momentum decreased by `1.343e-10 kg m/s`.

Logs: `no-slip-{build,tests,final-build,final-tests}.log` in the Debug build;
individual final output is in `Testing/Temporary/LastTest.log`. Provenance,
project-contract and whitespace checks passed. No Python source was changed.
No additional configuration matrix or external flow benchmark was run.

Moving/isothermal walls, prescribed inflow, AMR/EB and particle wall coupling
remain open; see the [boundary guide](../../cases/reactive_boundaries_3d/README.md).
