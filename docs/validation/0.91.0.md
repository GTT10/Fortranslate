# PeleF 0.91.0 validation record

## Scope

This milestone composes active-cell chemistry with the serial reactive EB AMR
lifecycle. An active hierarchy advances reaction half-steps on both levels
around the existing subcycled EB hydrodynamic transaction and synchronizes the
reacted fine state back to coarse. A root-only hierarchy reuses the qualified
single-level EB Strang path.

## Automated evidence

The direct driver gate proves that both coarse and fine chemistry half-steps can
succeed before an invalid hydro solver rejects the interval, while all four
state and temperature outputs remain bitwise equal to their inputs. A reacting
lifecycle gate advances first with a fine patch and then after conservative
collapse on the root, requires at least two accepted steps, and preserves total
mass and total energy within `3e-11` scaled tolerance. Molecular transport is
rejected before any time, step, or regrid counter advances.

The public regression advances the same uniform 1200 K elementary H2/O2 reactor
through the regular 8 by 8 application and through an EB AMR hierarchy with an
8 by 8 root and a ratio-two 10 by 10 fine patch. Its checker requires reaction
progress, all three EB cell classes on both levels, active species closure, and
coarse/fine density, velocity, pressure, temperature, total energy, and species
parity with the regular reference.

Implementation head `374f4db` is qualified by
[branch CI run 32830207461](https://github.com/GTT10/Fortranslate/actions/runs/32830207461)
in GNU Fortran Debug and Release configurations. All 164 tests passed in both;
Release completed in 185 seconds and Debug in 452 seconds. The test phases took
134.00 and 422.98 seconds respectively, with bounds, backtrace, and
floating-point exception checks enabled in Debug.

## Qualification boundary

Chemistry is qualified for one serial, ratio-aligned, strictly internal fine
rectangle over one root level, including fine-patch removal and root-only
continuation. EB AMR molecular transport, multiple patches, deeper levels,
checkpoint/restart, and distributed EB ownership remain outside this milestone.
