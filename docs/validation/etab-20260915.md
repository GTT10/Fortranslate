## Source and implementation boundary

The ETAB deformation and child-size equations are adapted from
[PelePhysics TABBreakup.H at the pinned revision](https://github.com/Pele-Suite/PelePhysics/blob/6e8026fc6ed121c07e32c9d3fe1d1c919bafc64b/Source/Spray/BreakupSplash/TABBreakup.H).
The downloaded header SHA256 is
`8b806c9fcb2305752f591cea4caa007484a31098e14e57e9cc50286bf2a29d10`.
The full upstream license is retained in `references/pelephysics-spray-license.txt`
and installed under `share/pelef/licenses`; the generated installation rule
was inspected, but a new install audit was not run. The source/license hashes
are captured in `references/spray_etab.json`.

This increment evolves deformation and its rate, applies the ETAB child-radius
law, and conserves represented mass by changing droplet mass and multiplicity.
Temperature and velocity remain fixed through the breakup operator. No new
computational child parcels or transverse velocity dispersion are generated;
the upstream transverse-velocity and parcel-splitting procedures remain open.
Liquid viscosity and surface tension are prescribed constants. Surface energy
remains outside the existing liquid energy convention.

Deliberate numerical adaptations: each oscillator substep is capped to the
remaining interval before evaluating the solution; zero relative velocity
avoids division by zero, overdamped states reset deformation as upstream, the
radius has the configured minimum, and invalid states/substep exhaustion roll
back the complete update. This is not an exact byte-for-byte upstream timestep
or stochastic-child equivalence claim.

Checkpoint V3/V4 adds both deformation scalars to the existing periodic/physical
formats. Breakup model/material parameters bind the context. V1/V2 remain
unchanged for runs without breakup; a writer refuses to silently discard
nonzero deformation when using a non-breakup format.

## Focused evidence

- `spray_etab`: the nonbreaking damped forced oscillator matches its analytic
  solution within relative 1e-13; an immediate high-We event matches the ETAB
  child-mass formula within 1e-12. A sustained high-We input decreases droplet
  mass and increases multiplicity while preserving mass/momentum/energy.
  Substep exhaustion rejects without changing the original parcel.
- `spray_etab_restart`: periodic and physical-boundary inert-water cases at
  10 MPa, 900 K and initial relative velocity 500 m/s. Stop after two steps and
  resume to 2 microseconds. Gas CSVs, parcel CSVs (including deformation) and
  final history rows match uninterrupted runs exactly. Represented liquid mass
  remains 1e-10 kg within relative 1e-12. The initial 20-micrometer diameter
  reaches approximately 0.1362344824 micrometers in this intentionally strong,
  frozen-relative-velocity fixture. This is an algorithm exercise, not a
  physical validation case; drag, heat transfer and evaporation are disabled.
- Together with existing `spray_integration_checkpoint` and
  `spray_application_cli`, 4/4 passed in 17.55 seconds. The oscillator unit was
  rerun after adding the exact child-size check and passed. The restart checker
  additionally rejects a changed surface tension.
- Project contract, pinned FFCM1_Red source provenance, Python syntax and
  `git diff --check` pass. Initial builds caught long Fortran lines and the new
  parcel components' constructor position; both were corrected before tests.
  The initial license install path used an unset directory variable; inspection
  caught it and it was changed to a prefix-relative destination before commit.
