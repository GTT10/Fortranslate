# PeleF 0.213.0 validation record

## Scope

This milestone adds zeroth-order weighted StateRedist to the qualified
single-level, static, exact-axis-plane 3D EB kernel. It keeps FluxRedist as a
separate comparison path and makes raw, FluxRedist, and StateRedist use one
common PCM face-flux and aperture/wall-divergence construction.

## Automated evidence

- x-, y-, and z-normal `kappa=0.05` cells reproduce the closed-form
  StateRedist cut and receiver values for three independent components;
- those directional cases require compact support, uniform active-state
  identity, zero standalone covered state, custom-target identity, and
  fluid-volume conservation for every component;
- invalid targets and nonfinite states publish zero, and a small cut cell at
  the domain edge with no regular receiver is rejected;
- the low-level NASA7 route redistributes a provisional negative-density cut
  state into physical cut and receiver states, recovers unchanged temperature
  for a uniformly scaled state, and conserves every Euler/species component;
- selecting `target=kappa` proves that redistribution is what restores
  admissibility, while a larger nonphysical step and all invalid requests roll
  back state and temperature exactly;
- the end-to-end hydro regression applies raw, FluxRedist, and StateRedist to
  the same common residual, repeats x/y/z uniform tangent-flow invariance, and
  checks nonuniform conservation, species closure, positive temperature,
  covered identity, and invalid solver/timestep/state/target transactions;
- at a three-dimensional CFL-0.5 full-grid step, the independently formed raw
  residual gives negative cut density and the raw route rejects, while both
  stabilization algorithms remain physical and conservative.

## Numerical signatures

For `kappa=0.05` and target volume `0.5`, the analytical provisional
`(-U, U)` pair produces cut factor `0.6363636363636364` and receiver factor
`0.9181818181818182`.

The end-to-end full-grid timestep is `2.7268814571340861e-05`. FluxRedist
finishes with cut density `8.7257250000000008e-01`; StateRedist finishes with
cut density `2.0784090909090919e-01`. The different values are expected because
one method redistributes the residual and the other redistributes the
provisional state.

The focused GNU Fortran 13.3.0 Debug regression reports maximum relative
uniform-state error `1.2727690974963220e-16`, zero uniform-temperature error,
maximum relative fluid-volume conservation error
`2.7755575615628914e-17`, and maximum species closure error
`5.5511151231257827e-17`.

## Qualification boundary

This record qualifies order-zero weighted StateRedist only for the exact
x/y/z axis-plane geometry, one axis-normal regular receiver, a serial
single-level PCM inviscid operator, and the current NASA7 general EOS. The
outer Cartesian boundary remains zero-gradient. FluxRedist remains available
as a separately named first-order algorithm and raw Euler remains a diagnostic
control.

It does not qualify oblique/curved or multi-neighbor geometry, higher-order
StateRedist, a public EB-aware timestep or application, characteristic cut-face
reconstruction, chemistry, molecular transport, AMR/reflux/regridding,
checkpoint/restart, MPI ownership, scalable I/O, or whole-application PeleC
parity.
