`2/8/24/32`, every parent-child restriction agrees within `5e-13`, and every
cell retains positive temperature and pressure, nonnegative species, and
species closure within `3e-10`.

strictly interior patch-tree PCM hydro. Molecular transport, dynamic tagging,
