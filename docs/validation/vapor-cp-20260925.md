Mixed fuel Cp is normalized over distinct mapped gas species; inactive mapped
fuel still contributes when present in the gas skin. Manually supplied films
with zero vapor Cp retain the former skin-Cp fallback. Negative or nonfinite
vapor Cp is rejected. AS and wall-film evaporation cap B_M at 20.

The latter differs from the reference B_T iteration; full coefficient and time
integrator parity is not claimed.
