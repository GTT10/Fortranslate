# PeleF 0.206.0 validation record

## Scope

This milestone adds molecular transport to the serial, single-level,
uniform-grid, periodic general-EOS three-dimensional application. The selected
subset contains Newtonian viscosity with Stokes' hypothesis, Fourier
conduction, mixture-averaged species diffusion, barodiffusion, correction
velocity, and species-enthalpy energy transport. Chemistry, transport, and
hydro compose transactionally as
`R(dt/2)-T(dt/2)-H(dt)-T(dt/2)-R(dt/2)`.

## Automated evidence

- `unit_reactive_transport_3d` compares complete transport SSPRK2 updates in
  x, y, and z with the established 2D operator for both the elementary
  seven-species and full-H2O2 ten-species tables, independently checks the 3D
  parabolic timestep, and proves exact failure rollback;
- `unit_reactive_chemistry_3d` additionally checks that transport-disabled
  full composition is bitwise identical to the previous Strang path, a
  transport-enabled uniform candidate succeeds, and a late invalid hydro
  solver cannot publish its valid transport prefix;
- `regression_reactive_transport_3d` measures a genuinely three-dimensional
  diagonal viscous shear mode and independently checks thermal and species
  smoothing, periodic integral conservation, and species positivity;
- `regression_reactive_transport_3d_control_run`,
  `regression_reactive_transport_3d_run`, and
  `regression_reactive_transport_3d_check` exercise the public full-H2O2
  configuration/driver/CSV path against an otherwise identical inert control;
- the prior reactive Euler, chemistry, hotspot, project-contract, binary-stack,
  Cantera, and MPI-enabled build gates remain in the full qualification set.

## Numerical signatures

For both mechanism tables, x- and z-dependent 3D SSPRK2 states matched the 2D
reference exactly. The y reduction had maximum component-scaled state error
`1.3427749528108081e-29`; all recovered temperatures matched exactly. The
species limiter remained one and the independently reconstructed stable step
was `6.54479996146319e-4 s`.

The 6^3, 12^3, and 24^3 diagonal shear-wave L1 errors were respectively
`8.018700e-6`, `1.900656e-6`, and `4.722475e-7`, giving observed orders
`2.076871` and `2.008883`. The separate conduction and species-diffusion
problems reduced their target span/variance while conserving periodic energy
or every individual species integral within their `2e-12` gates.

The public 6^3 full-H2O2 pair reached `t=2.0e-6 s` in ten steps. Relative to
the transport-disabled control, transport changed temperature by at most
`9.887e-2 K`, reduced its range by `9.890e-2 K`, and produced a maximum mass
fraction response of `1.698e-8`. The checker reported conserved-integral error
`2.776e-17`, primitive/EOS relationship error `4.309e-16`, and closure error
`3.331e-16`. The application reported maximum diffusivity
`1.3460684447580817e-3 m^2/s` and minimum species limiter one.

## Qualification boundary

This record qualifies the selected dilute-gas transport formulation and
full-physics split only on a serial periodic regular 3D grid. It does not
qualify Soret or Dufour effects, multicomponent diffusion, arbitrary
mechanism/transport ingestion, nonperiodic walls, production stiff-integrator
parity, high-order 3D hydro, AMR/reflux, restart, MPI decomposition, EB, LES,
particles/spray, scalable output, accelerators, external physical validation,
or whole-application PeleC parity.
