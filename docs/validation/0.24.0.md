# PeleF 0.24.0 validation record

Base: verified PeleF 0.23.0 distributed molecular transport.

## New subsystem

- rank-local general-EOS Rusanov hydro for the full 15-component reactive
  conserved state;
- transactional chemistry--transport--hydro--transport--chemistry composition;
- communicator-wide success decisions and complete rollback before timestep
  retry;
- a smooth, periodic, non-divisible 19-cell full-H2/O2 reacting-flow case.

## Acceptance gates

- complete gathered-field parity for 1, 2, 4, and 8 ranks within `5e-13`
  relative tolerance;
- positive finite density and temperature, nonnegative species, and composition
  closure;
- periodic conservation of mass, momentum, and total energy;
- nonzero state, temperature, and globally integrated chemistry response;
- successful Debug and Release MPI matrices plus the complete serial regression
  suite.

This milestone completes the planned one-dimensional MPI slice. It does not
claim multidimensional MPI, AMR, load balancing, or accelerator support.
