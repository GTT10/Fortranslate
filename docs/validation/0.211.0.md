# PeleF 0.211.0 validation record

## Scope

This milestone starts the three-dimensional embedded-boundary workstream with
one exact, static, axis-aligned plane on a single Cartesian level. Fluid lies
on the coordinate-positive side. The implementation supplies validated
cut-cell metrics, a general-EOS impermeable slip-wall pressure flux,
aperture-weighted conservative divergence, PCM zero-gradient outer-face
Riemann fluxes, and a transactional forward-Euler state/temperature update.

## Automated evidence

- the geometry unit checks fully regular and fully covered domains plus x-,
  y-, and z-normal cuts at `0.37` in a unit cube;
- the x-normal `10 x 8 x 6` case checks fluid volume `0.63`, 48 cut cells,
  cut fraction `0.30`, normalized cell and tangential-face centroid `0.35`,
  unit total EB area, physical EB centroids, and normal orientation;
- every geometry checks the cellwise equality of Cartesian open-face area
  divergence and the stored EB normal integral;
- corrupted face fractions, face centroids, and normal integrals are rejected,
  as are invalid bounds, extents, axes, nonfinite positions, and an interior
  grid-face-aligned plane;
- the wall unit checks an oblique unit normal, velocity-independent pressure
  traction, zero mass/energy/species wall flux, integrated x/y/z pressure
  force, uniform-pressure balance, covered-cell zero residual, and
  transactional invalid inputs;
- the regression advances uniform flow tangent to x/y/z planes with HLLC,
  Rusanov, and PeleC-style fluxes respectively, then advances a nonuniform
  density field and checks every fluid-volume-weighted conserved component,
  species closure, positive recovered temperature, covered-cell identity, and
  invalid-solver/timestep/state rollback.

## Numerical signatures

For all three plane orientations, the maximum normalized uniform-state error
and normalized recovered-temperature error are exactly zero in the GNU
Fortran 13.3.0 Release build. In the nonuniform Rusanov update, the maximum
relative change among all fluid-volume-weighted conserved components is
`2.7755575615628914e-17`; maximum species-density closure error is
`5.5511151231257827e-17`.

## Qualification boundary

This record qualifies the metric/index/sign convention and one conservative
general-EOS PCM Euler candidate for a serial, single-level, static,
non-grid-aligned axis plane. The physical-domain exterior state is
zero-gradient. The step contains no chemistry or molecular transport.

It does not qualify oblique or curved geometry, an interior EB coincident with
a Cartesian face, small-cell state or flux redistribution, characteristic
reconstruction, CTU/PPM, non-slip or thermal walls, AMR/reflux/regridding,
checkpoint/restart, MPI ownership, scalable I/O, a public 3D EB application,
or whole-application PeleC parity. Without redistribution, the stable
timestep is constrained by the smallest positive volume fraction.
