# PeleF 0.97.0 validation record

## Scope

This milestone extends reactive EB AMR outflow-side refinement from a
configured static rectangle to one-sided temperature tagging, dynamic single-
and multipatch planning, transactional topology changes, patch-set hydro, and
public output.

## Automated evidence

The planner unit gate places a temperature jump on an active root boundary
cell and requires an explicit boundary tag plus a domain-inclusive plan. A
direct collection gate accepts a boundary tag. The reactive multipatch gate
uses two separated plane-EB children, with the first on x-lower. It requires
subcycled hydro and Strang chemistry conservation, exact average-down
synchronization, and full rollback after a rejected solver. Moving that child
inward must retain every overlapping fine cell exactly, inject only newly
refined cells from the synchronized root, and preserve the composite integral.

The public 14 by 14 double-hotspot case plans one ratio-two child at x-lower
and one interior child at initialization and again after the accepted-step
regrid cadence. Its checker infers coarse bounds from fine coordinates and
requires exact domain alignment, ratio-two spacing, deterministic child order,
the two-cell separation contract, all EB classes across the fine set, retained
hotspots, requested final time, finite positive thermodynamics, and species
closure.

in GNU Fortran Release and bounds-, backtrace-, and

## Qualification boundary

The qualified physical sides use outflow conditions. The implementation does
not treat opposite sides as a periodic refinement seam and does not qualify
inflow, wall, catalytic, viscous, thermal, or mixed physical boundaries. EB
molecular transport, deeper EB levels, distributed ownership, and explicit
physical-side checkpoint/restart parity remain outside this milestone.
