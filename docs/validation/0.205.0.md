# PeleF 0.205.0 validation record

## Scope

This milestone adds optional cell-local chemistry to the serial, single-level,
uniform-grid, periodic general-EOS three-dimensional application. It supports
the built-in elementary seven-species explicit and full-H2O2 ten-species
implicit reactors and composes chemistry and hydro transactionally as
`R(dt/2)-H(dt)-R(dt/2)`. Molecular transport remains disabled.

## Automated evidence

- `unit_reactive_chemistry_3d` compares both 3D mechanism paths cell by cell
  with the established one-dimensional constant-volume reactor, checks H/O/N
  elemental conservation and nonzero chemistry, verifies uniform Strang
  reduction, and proves exact outer rollback after a valid reaction prefix and
  invalid hydro solver;
- `unit_reactive_euler_3d_dimensional_reduction` retains the nine
  Rusanov/HLLC/PeleC-style by x/y/z hydrodynamic reductions;
- `regression_reactive_entropy_wave_3d` and its public run/check pair retain
  the chemistry-disabled 3D hydrodynamic and dynamic-CSV contract;
- `regression_reactive_hotspot_3d_run` advances the public elementary
  chemistry/hydro hotspot;
- `regression_reactive_hotspot_3d_check` independently checks its exact dynamic
  schema, 216 rows, x-fastest coordinates and time, physical fields, EOS and
  conserved identities, species closure, analytical initial mass, H/O/N
  elemental totals, zero net momentum, nonzero chemistry, and temperature
  structure.

## Numerical signatures

The elementary 3D chemistry wrapper agreed with the established 1D cell
reactor to `2.7755575615628914e-17` in state and exactly in temperature. Its
maximum chemistry change was `4.8847233647473580e-6` and maximum H/O/N
elemental error was `4.3325044093918640e-16`.

The full-H2O2 wrapper agreed exactly in both state and temperature. Its maximum
chemistry change was `1.6963318281479614e-6` and maximum H/O/N elemental error
was `1.3430309482118420e-14`. Uniform 3D Strang reduction and whole-field
rollback were exact.

The public 6^3 hotspot completed five steps at `t=1.0e-6 s`. Temperature ranged
from `1200.0559331503277 K` to `1267.1242238713915 K`, maximum speed was
`1.5868119873961674e-2 m/s`, normalized Euler-integral change was
`1.9428902930940239e-16`, and maximum species-closure error was zero. The
independent CSV checker reported chemistry progress `2.806e-5`, mass error
`2.506e-16`, H/O/N elemental error `5.954e-15`, and closure error `1.307e-16`.

## Qualification boundary

This record qualifies internal H2/O2 reaction-source integration, splitting,
rollback, and conservation on the serial periodic 3D grid. The hotspot is not
an external ignition validation. This milestone does not qualify molecular
transport, direct arbitrary-mechanism ingestion, production stiff-integrator
parity, high-order 3D reconstruction, AMR, restart, MPI decomposition, EB,
LES, particles/spray, scalable output, accelerators, or whole-application
PeleC parity.
