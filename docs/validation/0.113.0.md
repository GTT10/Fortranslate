# PeleF 0.113.0 validation record

## Scope

This milestone executes two-level sibling-patch reactive EB molecular
transport on exclusive MPI physics owners. Each SSPRK2 Euler stage advances
the root once, ratio-subcycles every child on its owner, performs diffusive
reflux and EB-cut composite conservation closure, and synchronizes before the
next stage. Caller fields remain unchanged until the whole transaction is
accepted communicator-wide.

## Automated evidence

The MPI executable constructs a 14 by 14 plane-EB root and two separated
ratio-two 10 by 10 children with distinct density, temperature, and velocity.
Nonowners retain deliberately stale finite replicas. The serial multipatch EB
SSPRK2 path supplies the reference. The MPI path must reproduce its root and
child state and temperature fields within `2e-11` of their field scales and
its limiter minimum within `2e-13`, while producing a measurable transport
update.

The root physics owner records two Euler-level advances. Every child owner
records four fine advances, giving an exact communicator total of ten. Each
child owner accumulates coarse and fine time-integrated diffusive fluxes and
refluxes the authoritative root in both Euler stages. The root owner performs
the set-wide average-down and EB-cut conservation closure.

For the rollback gate, the final child owner receives a finite but EOS-invalid
density field. Root and earlier-child candidate work may occur first, but the
failure rejects all ranks. Every caller retains its original local root and
child fields bitwise and reports zero committed advances.

Implementation head `9ec30c7` is qualified by ordinary CI run 32901025313 and
MPI CI run 32901025256. The MPI workflow used Ubuntu 24.04 with OpenMPI and
ran the executable at one, two, four, and eight ranks in GNU Fortran Release
and bounds-, backtrace-, and floating-point-exception-checked Debug
configurations. All established MPI gates and the complete 208-test serial
regression passed in both MPI configurations. The ordinary Release and Debug
CI configurations independently passed all 208 tests.

## Qualification boundary

Chemistry, hydrodynamics, and molecular transport now execute on owners, while
EB geometry, patch topology, and complete field payloads remain replicated.
Sparse rank-local storage, decomposed root StateRedist, point-to-point traffic,
topology migration, parallel checkpoint I/O, and a public distributed EB AMR
time loop remain outside this milestone.
