# PeleF 0.27.0 validation record

## Scope

This milestone connects the two-level 1D AMR hierarchy to the reactive
general-EOS hydro and chemistry operators and supplies a runnable reacting
hotspot case.

## Automated evidence

`regression_amr_reactive_1d` checks:

- multiple accepted coarse steps and repeated regrid evaluations;
- a strictly interior active fine hierarchy;
- coarse/fine synchronization through restriction;
- hierarchy-wide thermodynamic positivity and species closure;
- retained hotspot temperature structure;
- composite mass, momentum, and energy conservation within `2e-10`.

`regression_amr_reactive_hotspot_run` executes
`pelef_amr_reactive_1d` from a namelist. Its structural checker verifies both
levels are present, the spacing ratio is two, composite coordinates are ordered,
cell widths cover the domain exactly once, the state is finite and positive,
species close, and a nontrivial thermal/pressure wave remains.

The complete existing serial suite remains active. The MPI-enabled workflow
also reruns the full serial suite before its 1/2/4/8-rank distributed gates.

## Qualification boundary

This is a two-level, single-patch, one-dimensional PCM qualification with
chemistry and without molecular transport. It is not yet a claim of high-order
AMR reconstruction, physical-boundary refinement, multiple patches, arbitrary
levels, AMR diffusion, MPI patch distribution, or load balancing.
