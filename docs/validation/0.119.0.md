## Scope

This milestone advances MPI reactive EB AMR hydro from sparse persistent
state. Fine children remain owner-local through subcycling, flux accumulation,
reflux, and final average-down.

## Automated evidence

The qualified 14 by 14 plane-EB root is distributed in y and has two separated
ratio-two 10 by 10 children with distinct nonuniform starting states. Direct
sparse hydro must retain each rank's exact stored-value count and match serial
multipatch hydro root and child state and temperature within `8e-12` field

ratio-subcycled advances. The rollback case places finite negative density on
the final child owner; root and earlier child candidates may execute, but every
local sparse field must remain bitwise unchanged and the published count must
be zero.

and MPI CI run `32909540110`. GNU Fortran Release and bounds/FPE-checked Debug

## Qualification boundary

Fine payloads are direct and sparse, but root start, updated state, temperature,
and flux arrays are still level-wide temporaries. Root StateRedist
decomposition, direct sparse transport, targeted point-to-point traffic,
public time-loop control, dynamic topology, checkpointing, and output remain
outside this milestone.
